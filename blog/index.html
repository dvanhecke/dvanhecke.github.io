FIRST AID AT GETTING HACKED

There is a big chance you got hacked if:

  • Your pc randomly slowed

  • You got a notification from your anti-virus

  • You notice programs you haven't installed yourself

In all of the above cases you should know you might've been hacked. In this blog I'll explain what to do if this is the case for you.

  1. First and foremost: dont panic because panic can give bad advice.

  2. Get your pc checked by a specialist who studied for this because this person will have experience about the matter

  3. Disconnect from the internet

  4. Change all your passwords, so the ones saved cant be used in case you got hacked.

  5. Let a specialist do a check for dataleaks with your data

  6. If you know for sure you got hacked, immediatly go to the police, hacking is a felony and must be told to the police.

  7. Check if you can make a backup of your files.

WHY TAKE A JOB IN ICT

Earlier people thought that an ICT'er was:

  • A boring and unatractive person

  • A so called 'grey' mouse, meaning a shy person

  • A wallflower, meaning someone who stays inside and never leaves

  • A person with no social life

  • Worldstrange

This is nothing but a bad image of ICT, now people start to realize ICT is youthfull, interesting and has lots of opportunities. Here are some examples:

  1. Workenvironement and flexibility

    In ICT you dont have to work in office, in fact you can work from anywhere there is an internet connection

  2. Work possibilities

    Society is evolving fast and so does technology as long as technology evolves there will always be people needed in ICT

  3. Salary

    Altough not the important fact on choosing a job. Companies pay a lot for employee's who are good educated in ICT because thats also what most companies get money from.

  4. Benifits

    Most companies give benefits when working there, but for ICT there are most of the time more benefits
